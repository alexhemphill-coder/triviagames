Step 1 — Set Up

Set up your league

Each manager takes over a struggling club and drafts a 20-man squad — one player from every Premier League club. In a multi-manager draft you take turns and no player can be picked twice, so race for the stars. Aim for 2 GK · 6 DEF · 6 MID · 6 FWD (a tracker keeps count). Then set a formation and XI, and we simulate the whole season.
