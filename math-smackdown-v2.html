How it works

Each player plays at their own grade level, so siblings of any age can battle fairly. On your turn, answer a question at your level for 11 damage, or be brave and reach up one level for 15 damage. Some questions are multiple choice; others ask you to type a number. Answer correctly to hurt your opponent — miss, and you deal nothing. First mathematician to knock the other to 0 HP wins.
