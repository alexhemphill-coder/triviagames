The Rules

Each player starts with 100 HP. On your turn, choose to answer a question at your home level for 11 damage, or reach up one tier for 15 damage. Wrong answers do nothing — but you learn something either way. Questions are a mix of multiple choice and typed answers (don't worry about exact spelling — close is fine). First to drop the opponent's HP to zero wins.
