🐾 How it works

Each player plays at their own age level, so siblings of any age can battle fairly. On your turn, answer a question at your level for 11 damage, or be brave and ⬆️ reach up one level for 15 damage. Some questions are multiple choice; others ask you to type an answer (spelling doesn't have to be perfect!). Answer correctly to knock down your opponent — miss, and you deal nothing. First champion to drop the other to 0 HP wins!
